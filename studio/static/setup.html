ONE-TIME PHONE SETUP

Put Image Studio on this phone.

Your Mac serves the studio securely, only on your home Wi-Fi. Trust your Mac once, then add the studio to your Home Screen.

Checking this phone…

  1. Download your Mac’s certificate Download certificate
    iPhone or iPad

    Tap Allow. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded, then Install. Next, go to General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on Image Studio Home.

    Android

    Open Settings and search for CA certificate. Choose Install anyway, then pick image-studio-home.crt from Downloads.

  2. Come back to this page

    It opens the studio as soon as this phone trusts your Mac.

    Open Image Studio
  3. Add it to your Home Screen

    iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app.

This certificate only works for home-network addresses.
