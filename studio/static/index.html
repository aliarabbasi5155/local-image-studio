Install it as an app

The first time, this address walks your phone through trusting your computer, then opens the secure studio. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, choose Install app from the ⋮ menu. It reaches your computer only on your home Wi-Fi.

Away from home

The installed app keeps a copy of your library on your phone. Away from home you can browse, search, compare, favorite, archive, delete and download PNGs, and play saved clips. Your changes reach your computer when you’re back on your home Wi-Fi. Creating, editing, selections, identity swaps, videos and JPG or ZIP downloads need your computer. Lock studio removes the copy from your phone.

Send photos from Share

On Android, share a PNG, JPEG or WebP photo to Image Studio, then review and import it. On iPhone, use a Shortcut to send photos to your computer.

A few useful basics

Each library card is a project: an original with all its edits and clips. Tap Images at the top to see every image instead. Open a card to land on the image it shows.

In the viewer, swipe to move between images and projects, pinch or double-tap to zoom, and tap once to hide the controls. Press and hold the photo to see what it came from, or turn on Compare to drag a split between the two.

Edit describes a change to the whole photo; Area lets you paint or find the part to change; Person swaps in someone else; Animate makes a short clip. Fast is a quick start; Balanced and High use the more detailed model.

Size, resizing, images per edit and SNOFS are in Settings. Downloads are JPGs. Select cards in the library to favorite, download or delete many at once.
